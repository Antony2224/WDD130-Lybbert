Frequently Asked Questions

Question Answer
Where is data stored? Servers are hosted privately with an end to end connection between your main computer in your place of business and the server database here at YourSource.
How are servers safer? Thanks to our hosting procedures your data couldn't be safer. It is not run through the unprotected cloud or stored in the cloud. This and the end-to-end encryption let you rest easy, free from the fear of loss of personal data from hacks or malicious data miners in the cloud.
What if I lose my connection to the server? Is there a risk of data loss? No, if anything happens, there is no risk of data loss. Everything is backed up on a personal drive designated for you and a backup in the server if the drive fails. In our database, your data is not mixed with others or analyzed by our backend machines.
